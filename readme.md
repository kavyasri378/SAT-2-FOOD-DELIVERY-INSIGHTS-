# Food Delivery Insight: Data-Driven Analysis of Delivery Performance

## Project Overview
Food Delivery Insight is a data analytics project that analyzes food delivery operations to identify the factors affecting delivery time and operational efficiency. The project uses Excel to organize data, perform statistical analysis, create PivotTables, and visualize key insights through charts.

## Objectives
- Analyze average delivery time across different zones.
- Compare delivery times under different traffic conditions.
- Study the impact of weather on delivery performance.
- Identify restaurants with higher pickup times.
- Analyze the relationship between delivery distance and delivery time.
- Identify peak ordering hours and their impact on delivery performance.
- Calculate statistical measures to understand the dataset.

## Tools and Technologies
- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Excel formulas and statistical functions
- Data cleaning and data visualization

## Key Analysis
1. **Delivery Time by Zone:** Compares average delivery time across delivery zones.
2. **Traffic Analysis:** Examines delivery time under low, medium, and high traffic conditions.
3. **Weather Impact:** Analyzes delivery time across different weather conditions.
4. **Restaurant Pickup Analysis:** Identifies restaurants with higher average pickup times.
5. **Distance Analysis:** Studies the relationship between delivery distance and delivery time.
6. **Peak-Hour Analysis:** Examines delivery performance during different ordering hours.
7. **Statistical Analysis:** Summarizes key metrics using mean, median, mode, standard deviation, variance, minimum, maximum, and range where applicable.

## Project Files
- **ROUND_3_SAT2_with_Statistical_Data.xlsx** – Excel workbook containing the analysis, charts, summary, and statistical data.
- **Cleaned Dataset** – Prepared dataset used for analysis, available within the workbook if included.
- **Summary Sheet** – Presents key findings and business insights.
- **Statistical Data Sheet** – Contains descriptive statistics and important delivery metrics.

## Expected Outcomes
- Understand the major factors influencing delivery time.
- Identify traffic, weather, distance, and pickup-related challenges.
- Discover peak-hour delivery patterns.
- Support data-driven decisions to improve delivery efficiency and customer satisfaction.

## Conclusion
The Food Delivery Insight project demonstrates how Excel-based data analysis and visualization can help understand food delivery performance. The findings can support better delivery planning, improved operational efficiency, and more informed business decisions.
